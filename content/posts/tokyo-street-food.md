## A Food Lover's Paradise

Tokyo is a haven for food enthusiasts, and its street food scene is absolutely incredible. From bustling markets to quiet alleyways, amazing food awaits around every corner.

### Best Street Food Areas

#### Shibuya

The heart of Tokyo's youth culture also offers fantastic street food options. Don't miss:

- Takoyaki stands
- Crepe shops
- Yakitori skewers

#### Asakusa

This traditional neighborhood near Senso-ji Temple has streets lined with food vendors:

- Freshly made ningyo-yaki
- Melon pan
- Traditional Japanese sweets

### Must-Try Dishes

**Takoyaki**: Octopus balls covered in savory sauce and bonito flakes. These are addictive!

**Yakitori**: Grilled chicken skewers seasoned with salt or tare sauce. Perfect with a cold beer.

**Taiyaki**: Fish-shaped cake filled with sweet red bean paste or custard.

**Okonomiyaki**: Savory pancake loaded with cabbage, meat, and seafood.

### Eating Etiquette

Remember these important tips:

1. Don't eat while walking (find a spot to stand)
2. Dispose of trash properly
3. Cash is preferred at small vendors
4. Basic Japanese phrases go a long way

### Hidden Gems

Some of my favorite discoveries:

- A tiny yakitori stand in Nakano that's been operating for 50 years
- A taiyaki shop in Kagurazaka with unique fillings
- Late-night ramen carts in Shinjuku

### Budget Tips

Street food in Tokyo is incredibly affordable:

- Takoyaki: ¥300-500
- Yakitori: ¥100-200 per skewer
- Taiyaki: ¥150-300

You can have a satisfying street food dinner for under ¥1500 ($10).

## Conclusion

Tokyo's street food scene offers an authentic taste of Japanese culture. Whether you're on a budget or just love trying new foods, the city's streets have something delicious to offer at every turn.
