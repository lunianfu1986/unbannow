# To the Ubisoft Player Support Team

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing with a heavy heart and a sincere plea regarding the recent permanent suspension of my Rainbow Six Siege account. I have invested thousands of hours and a considerable amount of personal savings into this game; for me, Siege is not merely a pastime—it is a treasured part of my daily life, a place where I bond with friends across continents, and a community that has helped me navigate difficult times. Unfortunately, my account was banned for alleged cheating or 

![To the Ubisoft Player Support Team](/uploads/dota-2-unban-appeal-guide-825x458.webp "To the Ubisoft Player Support Team")

unauthorized behavior—an allegation I firmly believe is a mistake arising either from an account compromise or a data‐processing error on the platform. I respectfully request a thorough re‑examination of my case and the swift restoration of my account.From the day I first installed Rainbow Six Siege, I have taken great pride in playing within the spirit of fair competition. I have never used third‑party tools, macros, or any exploits to gain an advantage. The joy for me has always come from mastering each Operator’s unique mechanics, coordinating strategic pushes with teammates, and refining my reflexes through countless rounds of Ranked, Quick Play, and custom scrimmages. My gameplay history shows a consistent progression in skill, never a sudden and implausible spike that might indicate foul play. The statistics associated with my in‑game performance—moderate win / loss ratio, gradual improvement, and a realistic headshot percentage—are entirely in line with a dedicated but fair competitor.Prior to the ban, I experienced several suspicious login events. I noticed unfamiliar IP addresses in the recent‐activity logs, and I received two “new device” e‑mail alerts in a language I do not speak. At the time, I assumed these were harmless glitches, yet in retrospect they point strongly toward an account breach. Furthermore, friends who attempted to party up with me during those days reported seeing my profile status flicker online and offline at odd hours—times when I was asleep or at work. I am prepared to submit my entire archive of Ubisoft Connect security emails, screenshots of the login anomalies, and statements from fellow players who witnessed these irregularities. I am likewise willing to provide any additional logs or verification steps you deem necessary to confirm that these activities did not originate from me.The emotional impact of this suspension has been profound. Siege has long been my primary form of recreation, stress relief, and social connection. Late‐night ranked sessions helped quiet my anxiety after demanding workdays, while weekend tournaments with my friends fostered camaraderie that spilled over into real‑life friendships. With the ban in place, I have been abruptly cut off from relationships that mean a great deal to me. The sudden removal of the game from my life has genuinely affected my mental health, to the point where I find myself battling bouts of discouragement and depressive thoughts. I share this not as a bid for sympathy, but to illustrate how much this community means to me and how devastating an unwarranted suspension can be.
