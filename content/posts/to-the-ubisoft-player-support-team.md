# To the Ubisoft Player Support Team

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing with a heavy heart and a sincere plea regarding the recent permanent suspension of my Rainbow Six Siege account. I have invested thousands of hours and a considerable amount of personal savings into this game; for me, Siege is not merely a pastime—it is a treasured part of my daily life, a place where I bond with friends across continents, and a community that has helped me navigate difficult times. Unfortunately, my account was banned for alleged cheating or unauthorized behavior—an allegation I firmly believe is a mistake arising either from an account compromise or a data‐processing error on the platform. I respectfully request a thorough re‑examination of my case and the swift restoration of my account.From the day I first installed Rainbow Six Siege, I have taken great pride in playing within the spirit of fair competition. I have never used third‑party tools, macros, or any exploits to gain an advantage. The joy for me has always come from mastering each Operator’s unique mechanics, coordinating strategic pushes with teammates, and refining my reflexes through countless rounds of Ranked, Quick Play, and custom scrimmages. My gameplay history shows a consistent progression in skill, never a sudden and implausible spike that might indicate foul play. The statistics associated with my in‑game performance—moderate win / loss ratio, gradual improvement, and a realistic headshot percentage—are entirely in line with a dedicated but fair competitor.Prior to the ban, I experienced several suspicious login events. I noticed unfamiliar IP addresses in the recent‐activity logs, and I received two “new device” e‑mail alerts in a language I do not speak. At the time, I assumed these were harmless glitches, yet in retrospect they point strongly toward an account breach. Furthermore, friends who attempted to party up with me during those days reported seeing my profile status flicker online and offline at odd hours—times when I was asleep or at work. I am prepared to submit my entire archive of Ubisoft Connect security emails, screenshots of the login anomalies, and statements from fellow players who witnessed these irregularities. I am likewise willing to provide any additional logs or verification steps you deem necessary to confirm that these activities did not originate from me.The emotional impact of this suspension has been profound. Siege has long been my primary form of recreation, stress relief, and social connection. Late‐night ranked sessions helped quiet my anxiety after demanding workdays, while weekend tournaments with my friends fostered camaraderie that spilled over into real‑life friendships. With the ban in place, I have been abruptly cut off from relationships that mean a great deal to me. The sudden removal of the game from my life has genuinely affected my mental health, to the point where I find myself battling bouts of discouragement and depressive thoughts. I share this not as a bid for sympathy, but to illustrate how much this community means to me and how devastating an unwarranted suspension can be.Financially, I have supported Rainbow Six Siege since Year 1 with a considerable sum—season passes, cosmetic bundles, Operator unlocks, and esports team charms. My transaction history is proof of my ongoing commitment to the title and its developers. I make these purchases not out of necessity, but because I believe in supporting a game I love and the hardworking team behind it. The prospect of losing access to every Operator, skin, and charm I earned or purchased is disheartening beyond words.It is precisely because I value the integrity of Siege that I uphold its rules without compromise. In the unfortunate event that any malicious actions were executed from my account while it was compromised, I kindly ask that you use your investigative tools to separate my legitimate activity from any unauthorized behavior. I plead for a chance to prove my innocence and clear my name. I am fully prepared to comply with additional security requirements, such as two‑factor authentication or identity verification, to ensure that any potential breach is permanently closed.I understand that your policies against cheating and misconduct are essential to preserving a fair competitive environment, and I deeply respect the vigilance required to enforce them. However, I also believe that the principles of fairness and due diligence warrant a thorough review when someone with a long, clean record appeals a sudden ban. My previous submissions may have lacked certain pieces of evidence or context, and I apologize if that hindered your ability to verify my claim. With this letter, I have consolidated all relevant information, articulated the timeline of suspicious activity, and reaffirmed my willingness to cooperate in any manner you deem necessary.Restoring my account would allow me to return to the game I love, continue supporting its growth, and help foster the welcoming atmosphere that attracted me to Siege in the first place. I take seriously the responsibility of setting a positive example for newer players, promoting teamwork, sportsmanship, and adherence to Ubisoft’s Code of Conduct. If my account is reinstated, I will redouble my efforts to maintain a secure account, safeguard my credentials, and report any vulnerabilities I encounter. I will also continue to invest in Siege—both in time and in the purchase of new content—because I firmly believe in the future of this franchise.Thank you for taking the time to read my appeal. I understand the volume of requests you must process and I appreciate the difficulty of distinguishing genuine players from malicious actors. Nevertheless, I hold faith in Ubisoft’s commitment to accuracy, transparency, and player satisfaction. I respectfully ask that you grant my case a close, individualized review and provide me with any specific evidence or findings that led to the suspension. Such transparency will not only clarify my situation but also strengthen community trust in your anti‑cheat processes.Should you need further documentation, testimony, or real‑time communication to resolve this matter, please do not hesitate to contact me. I am eager to provide whatever is required to demonstrate my integrity and restore my rightful place within the Rainbow Six community.Thank you again for your time and understanding. I look forward to your positive response and hope to once more join my friends in defending objectives, strategizing new pushes, and celebrating the unique tactical brilliance that only Rainbow Six Siege offers.Sincerely
