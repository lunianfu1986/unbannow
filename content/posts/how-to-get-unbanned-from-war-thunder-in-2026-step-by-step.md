**Most War Thunder account bans boil down to one of four common scenarios. Before we dive into the solution, it is important to identify which category you fall into:**

**1. Cheating / Use of Third-Party Software**

This is the most frequent cause. If the system flags you for using hacks, the ban is typically issued after a manual review. While these bans are usually accurate, system errors do happen. If you believe this is a false positive caused by a technical glitch, you can—and should—appeal.

**2. Account Trading (RMT)**

If Gaijin suspects you of buying or selling an account, the situation is difficult. The War Thunder Terms of Service explicitly forbid account trading. Unfortunately, if this is the reason for your ban, the chances of getting unbanned are extremely slim.

**3. Compromised Account (Hacked)**

If your account was stolen and the hacker used cheats while playing on it, you have a strong case. Since the cheating activity wasn't your fault, support can often verify the unauthorized access and lift the ban. Note that Gaijin usually offers this type of amnesty only once.

**4. Conflicting Software on Your PC**

Sometimes, you might be innocent of cheating in War Thunder, but the anti-cheat software detects cheat programs for *other* games on your computer (such as *Delta Force* or *Escape from Tarkov*). Even if you didn't use them in War Thunder, their presence alone can trigger a ban.

**If you are facing any of these issues, filing an appeal is your only path forward. So, how exactly do you navigate the process? Below, I have outlined a detailed, step-by-step guide to help you submit your appeal and hopefully recover your account.**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About War Thunder Bans

### Q1: How long does it take for Gaijin Support to respond to a ban appeal?
**Answer:** Typically, Gaijin Support responds within **2 to 3 business days**. However, during ban waves or holiday periods, response times can extend up to **5-7 days**. Please note that submitting multiple tickets for the same issue ("spamming") will push your request to the bottom of the queue, delaying the process further.

### Q2: Can I get unbanned if my account was hacked and the hacker used cheats?
**Answer:** Yes, it is possible. Gaijin has a policy that may allow for a **one-time amnesty** if you can prove your account was compromised at the time of the offense. You will need to provide evidence such as login history from a different location/IP address. If verified, they may secure your account and lift the ban, but this is usually a one-time exception.

### Q3: What does a "Negative Golden Eagles" balance mean and how do I fix it?
**Answer:** A negative Golden Eagles (GE) balance usually occurs due to a **payment chargeback** (refunding a purchase through your bank after spending the currency) or buying GE from unauthorized third-party sites. To unban your account, you must **repay the debt**. You need to contact Gaijin Support, who will calculate the amount owed and provide a link to settle the negative balance.

### Q4: Will I get banned for using mods, sound packs, or custom skins?
**Answer:** Generally, **cosmetic mods** from the official *War Thunder Live* portal (like custom skins, sights, and sound mods) are safe. However, using unauthorized third-party software that modifies game files, reveals enemy locations (ESP), or automates gameplay (bots) will trigger the **Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC)** system and lead to a permanent ban. Always download mods from official sources only.

### Q5: Is it possible to appeal a permanent ban for "Unsporting Behavior"?
**Answer:** "Unsporting Behavior" in War Thunder often refers to violations like botting, team-killing, or exploiting game mechanics. While difficult to overturn, you can appeal if you believe it was a false positive caused by software conflicts (e.g., anti-cheat flagging innocent background programs like RGB controllers or other game trainers). Be prepared to send your **DxDiag** and **game logs** (.clog files) to prove your innocence.
