## What Gets Your Account Banned in Escape from Tarkov?

1. **229 cheating ban, caused by using unauthorized third-party cheat programs; in this case it is basically very difficult to get the ban lifted.**
2. **Maliciously exploiting in-game glitches / bugs.**
3. **Real-money trading (RMT: buying or selling roubles / gear / boosting services, etc.).**
4. **Your account was stolen and then used by someone else to cheat.**
5. **There are cheat programs for other games on your computer, which are detected by Tarkov’s anti-cheat system and result in a ban.**

## Escape from Tarkov Ban FAQ

### How long does Escape from Tarkov customer support take to reply to an appeal letter?

Under normal circumstances, they will respond within three to five days. This depends on the progress of the support team in handling cases, and sometimes, during large-scale ban waves, the response speed will slow down because there are not enough support staff.

### Is the attitude of Tarkov customer support very bad when they reply?

No. After investigating, the support staff usually use very polite and proper wording. They will clearly tell you which specific rule was used to ban your account—for example, sometimes they will tell you that it was due to a violation of clause 6.14.

### If the appeal fails, can you still continue submitting appeal letters through the official Tarkov website’s customer support?

Yes, you can. If the first time appeal fails, you can still keep sending appeal letters, but you should change the content of your appeal and use touching, heartfelt wording to tell customer support that you really want your account to be restored.
