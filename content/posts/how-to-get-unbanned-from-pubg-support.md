There are many reasons that lead to PUBG bans, but most bans are caused by using cheats or other reasons. Some are caused by account theft. If it is a genuine case of account theft, there is still a chance to appeal and recover it. Please do not give up this opportunity. These are the steps to appeal PUBG in 2026. Please read the detailed steps inside carefully.

Based on current information, here are the main reasons for getting banned in PUBG:

## Primary Ban Reasons:

**1. Cheating/Hacking**

* Using unauthorized programs, cheats, hacks, or third-party software
* Using macros or scripts to gain unfair advantages
* This results in permanent bans

**2. Teaming**

* Cooperating with enemy players in Solo mode
* Forming alliances with opponents to eliminate other players
* Violates fair play rules

**3. Toxic Behavior**

* Offensive, racist, or discriminatory language
* Inappropriate usernames
* Harassment and verbal abuse
* Usually temporary bans, but can be permanent for severe cases

**4. Playing with Hackers**

* Repeatedly teaming up with known cheaters to boost rank
* Even unknowingly playing with hackers multiple times can result in bans

**5. Account Violations**

* Account trading or selling
* Account boosting services
* Using multiple accounts for unfair advantages
* Unauthorized account access

**6. Team Killing**

* Intentionally killing your own teammates
* Results in temporary or permanent bans

   ## FAQ

   **1. How long does the PUBG appeal process take?**

      Usually, there will be a reply in 2-3 days. Sometimes it's faster, it could be a few minutes or a few hours, depending on the review progress of the appeal customer service.

   **2. Can a permanent ban be lifted through appeal?**

      Yes, if you were indeed banned due to account theft, there is a chance to appeal and get it back. Please believe in yourself!
